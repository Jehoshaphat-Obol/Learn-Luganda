# 2026-10-21: Hearts

Issue: #16 · Branch: `feat/16-hearts`

## Context

Hearts make each answer matter: guessing costs something, and running out
nudges learners toward practice instead of grinding new lessons.

## Done

- `src/state/hearts.js`: 5 hearts; `loseHeart`, `gainHeart`,
  `refillHearts` (40 gems), `settleHearts` and a read-only `heartStatus`
  with the time the next heart arrives. One heart returns every 4 hours,
  computed from `hearts.updatedAt` whenever hearts are read, carrying the
  remainder so no time is lost. `formatWait` for "3h 12m".
- Lessons run with `hearts: true`: a heart counter sits top right, a wrong
  answer costs one (with a wobble animation), and the top bar updates.
- Running out mid-lesson opens a non-dismissible choice: refill for gems
  (if affordable), practise to earn hearts, or end the lesson. Refilling
  resumes the lesson where it was.
- Starting a lesson with no hearts shows an "Out of hearts" screen with the
  wait time and the same options.
- The runner stays heart-free by default, so practice and review sessions
  (#18) won't cost hearts.
- 6 unit tests: losing, floor at zero, timed refills with remainder,
  read-only status, gain/refill, formatting.

## Decisions

- Refill cost 40 gems against a 50-gem start: one rescue is possible on
  day one, and gems from #19 make it a real choice later.
- The refill clock starts at the first lost heart, like Duolingo.
