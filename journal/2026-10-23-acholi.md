# 2026-10-23 — Acholi (#130)

## What was done

- **Acholi as one `lang/ach` pack** in the shared catalog (#184), registered in `languages.json` and offered in Uganda (`regions.json`). It composes with every other pack: courses run from Acholi to Luganda, Swahili, French, Runyankore and Lusoga, and from each of them to Acholi. English to Acholi works too.
- **All 40 units** (`lang/ach/units/u1–u40.json`) and the course file, with the section, unit and skill titles, cast names (Lakica, Okot, Adaa Lanyero, Opiyo, Atim) and places (Gulu, Kitgum, Pader, Karuma, Lamwo, Atiak). Every pairing composes all 40 units and 462 lessons with no errors.
- **English notes about Acholi** for every unit (`lang/en/about/ach/units`): intros, grammar tips, culture notes and word notes.
- **Landing copy about Acholi** in English, Luganda, Swahili, French, Runyankore and Lusoga. **Landing copy in Acholi** about Luganda, Swahili, French, Runyankore and Lusoga.
- Acholi added to the language names in the four UI packs and in every lang pack; the language catalog now marks it as in the registry.
- The full picker ("Something else") used to start on whichever language sorted first, which became Acholi. It now starts on the guessed language, else English.

## How it was drafted

Acholi is a Luo language, so nothing could be carried over from the Bantu packs. Every line was translated from the English curriculum by Claude, with low confidence, and the course is marked `draft`. Main choices: *Itye nining? / Atye maber*, *Apwoyo*, numbers *acel… apar, apar wiye acel, pyeraryo, miya, alip*, days *Ceng Baraja… Ceng Cabit*, East African time (*cawa acel* is 7am), future *bi-*, negation *pe*, "let's" *wa-*, plural commands *wu-*, perfect *dong*, past state *onongo*, relative *ma*, comparison *loyo*, conditional *ka onongo… kono*.

## Doubts for a native-speaker reviewer

- Tone and vowel length are not marked anywhere; check spellings that differ only in these.
- Day names and East African time expressions.
- Borrowed words (*takci, bengi, netwak, aksidan*) versus the Acholi words people actually use.
- The Ganda proverbs and the Kintu story retold in Acholi (units 36 and 40).
- The Luganda, Swahili, Runyankore, Lusoga and French landing copy about Acholi.
