## How it is drafted

Ateso is an Eastern Nilotic language, so every line is translated from the English curriculum by Claude, with low confidence; the course is marked `draft`. The choices (verb prefixes, *abu* past, *abeit* future, *mam* negation, numbers, days, East African time) are listed in the project handoff folder (`handoff/teo/GLOSSARY.md`) so later units stay consistent.

## Doubts for a native-speaker review

- Weekdays: Saturday is *Nakaikanykape* and Sunday *Sabiti*, as in u5; later units follow that.
- Words chosen with low confidence: *ateker* for clan, *etal* for totem, *ekaitotan* for relative, *erwosit* for king, *alemikin* for proverb, *ailimor* for story, *akiosikin* for respect, *ekagielan* for customer. The Luganda proverbs in u36 are translated literally.
- The *-kini* (for someone) and *-akini* (make someone do) verb forms in u27 and u31 are guesses from the pattern.
