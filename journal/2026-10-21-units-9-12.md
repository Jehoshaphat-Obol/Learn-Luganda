# 2026-10-21: Units 9 to 12

Issue: #51 · Branch: `feat/51-units-9-12`

## Done

Section 2 (Everyday life) opens with four units. Each has 3 skills × 3
lessons, a guidebook and a checkpoint. The course is now 12 units, 89
lessons and 282 words.

| Unit | Skills | Guidebook tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 Colours and describing | Colours, Big and small, Describing people | Two kinds of colour word, “is” vs “the red one”, people adjectives |
| 10 Clothes | What I wear, Getting dressed, Buying clothes | “My” with n- nouns (yange/zange), “this” (eno), ngitwala |
| 11 My day | Waking up, At work and home, Evening | Subject prefixes n-/o-/a-/tu-/mu-/ba-, ku- infinitive |
| 12 Weather and seasons | Sun and rain, Seasons, Hot and cold | Weather verbs agree with their noun, ttoggo and ekyeya |

- New stories: *A gomesi for the party* (Unit 10) and *A rainy afternoon*
  (Unit 12).
- Section 2 guidebooks and stories live in `src/content/guidebooks/` and
  `src/content/stories/`, one file per section, so the original files stay
  readable as the course grows.
- **Match fix**: match exercises now never pair two words with the same
  English or Luganda (e.g. *mulungi* and *kirungi*, both “good”), which
  would be ambiguous. A test checks every lesson.
- New e2e test: a learner takes the Section 2 test, passes, and lands on
  Unit 9 with Section 1 shown as complete. axe now also checks the Unit 9
  guidebook, the gomesi story and a Unit 10 lesson.
