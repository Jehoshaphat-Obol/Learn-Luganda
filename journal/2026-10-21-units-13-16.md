## Decisions

- Sentence ids from Unit 13 on carry the unit number (`s13-…`) so they
  can’t clash across 40 units.
- *ennyonyi* (aeroplane) and *ekinyonyi* (bird) are taught in different
  units with a note, since learners mix them up.
