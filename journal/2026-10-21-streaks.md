# 2026-10-21: Streaks

Issue: #17 · Branch: `feat/17-streaks`

## Context

A streak turns "learn a little every day" into something visible and
worth protecting, Duolingo's strongest habit loop.

## Done

- `src/state/streak.js`:
  - `extendStreak` counts today once (first lesson of the day), updates
    the longest streak and flags milestones (3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200,
    365 days).
  - `settleStreak` handles missed days: if streak freezes cover every
    missed day they're used up and logged in `frozenDays`; otherwise the
    streak resets to 0. It runs when the app opens, so the top bar is
    never stale.
  - `streakStatus` (read-only): current, longest, freezes, done today, at
    risk.
- `finishLesson` extends the streak in the same update as XP; the rewards
  sequence adds a streak screen (with `streakScene`) on the first lesson of
  the day, with confetti on milestones and a "N more to reach M" nudge.
- Top bar flame is grey until today's lesson is done and links to the new
  `/streak` page: current and longest streak, freezes owned (max 2),
  progress to the next milestone, and a month calendar marking practised
  days (vermillion) and freeze days (snowflake).
- Learning path: an "Your N day streak is at risk!" banner while today is
  not done; the sidebar streak card links to `/streak`.
- 7 unit tests: daily extension, reset, freezes covering gaps, freezes not
  covering gaps, at-risk, month boundaries, milestones.

## Decisions

- Any finished lesson (not the full daily goal) extends the streak, so a
  busy day still counts; the daily goal stays its own target.
- The at-risk banner shows all day rather than only in the evening;
  there's no server to send a nudge at the right hour.
- Freezes are bought in the shop (#19).
