## Native-speaker review checklist (Units 37–40)

- [ ] News: *amawulire*, *Owulidde amawulire?*, *Mpulidde ku leediyo*,
      *Agamba nti enkuba ejja*, *Bagamba nti ekkubo eriggya liwedde*,
      *olukiiko*, *ssentebe*, *Ndowooza nti kirungi*, *Endowooza yo ki?*,
      *Nzikiriza naawe*, *Sikkiriza*, *Ndowooza nti tusimbe emiti ku
      kyalo*.
- [ ] Emergencies: *Nnyamba!*, *Yanguwa!*, *Omuliro!*, *Omubbi!
      Mumukwate!*, *Kubira poliisi essimu!*, *Essimu yange ebbiddwa*,
      *akabenje*, *alumiziddwa*, *Alina omusaayi* for “he is bleeding”,
      *ambulensi*, *Tukubire ambulensi mangu*, *Tuula wano, linda*,
      *Ndi bulungi*, *Tewali kizibu*; the passive tip (*okubbibwa*,
      *okulumizibwa*); emergency numbers 999 / 112.
- [ ] Respect: *okufukamira*, *Abaana bafukamira nga babuuza abakadde*,
      *Mwasuze mutya, ssebo?*, *Mwebale nnyo, nnyabo*, *Tulina okuwa
      abakadde ekitiibwa*, *Mwattu, mpa amazzi*, *Webale okujja! Tusanyuse
      okukulaba*, *Omugenyi ye mukulu* (is this a real saying?), *Tuula,
      olyeko*, *Yingira otuule*, *Mugenda wa?*; who kneels to whom.
- [ ] Folktales: *Wakayima*, *omugezi*, *yalimba*, *akakodyo*, *Kintu ye
      muntu eyasooka*, *Kintu yawasa Nnambi, muwala wa Ggulu*, *Nnambi
      yeerabira obulo bw’enkoko*, *Walumbe n’abagoberera*, *Enfumo eno
      etuyigiriza ki?*, *Etuyigiriza nti obugezi businga amaanyi*; the
      retelling of the legend in the guidebook.
- [ ] Stories: *Kiki ekibaddewo?*, *adduse n’agenda eri*, *Olumiziddwa?*,
      *ntidde*, *Poliisi egamba nti ejja mangu*, *Ky’omukwano*, *Yabeeranga
      yekka n’ente ye*, *n’amwagala*, *Mugende mangu, Walumbe aleme
      okubagoberera*, *Okuva olwo, abantu bafa*, *tuwulirize abakadde*.
- [ ] Course-complete screen: *Wayita bulungi* as “well done / you made
      it”.
