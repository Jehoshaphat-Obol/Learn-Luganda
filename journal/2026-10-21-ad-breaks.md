# 2026-10-21: Ad breaks (#95)

## Asked

Let learners opt out of ads for a while, free, with some personality: the
app should let them know, playfully, that ads keep it free.

## Done

- `src/ads/breaks.js`: take, check and end a break; kept on the device
  (`learn-luganda:ad-breaks`). `allowAd` says no while a break runs.
- Rules in the frozen policy (`adBreak` per level): length, breaks per
  rolling week, a longer break once the streak reaches 7 days. Gentle: 24 h,
  3 a week, 48 h with a streak. Tests bound every level.
- "Ad break" pill on every ad card; the crane's modal offers the break
  ("These sponsored cards pay for my birdseed…"), shows breaks left, and a
  thank-you toast. When none are left it says when the next one lands.
- Settings › Ads and privacy: status, Take an ad break, Bring ads back early.
- All copy in `src/ads/strings.js`, like the reminder strings, ready for
  the language config.

## Decisions

- Breaks can't stack; ending early still uses one, so the weekly cap means
  something.
- The pill sits in the card header, away from the ad itself, so it can't
  be mistaken for part of the ad or cause clicks on it.
