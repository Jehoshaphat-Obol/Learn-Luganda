# 2026-10-21: Units 3 to 8

Issue: #25 · Branch: `feat/25-units-3-8`

## Done

The course now has 8 units, 53 lessons and 198 words. The six new units
each have three skills, a guidebook and a checkpoint, and two of them have
a new story.

| Unit | Skills | Guidebook tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 Food and the market | Staple foods, Eating and drinking, At the market | Staple and sauce, “I have” for feelings, n-/o- verbs |
| 4 Numbers and money | Numbers 11–100, Shillings, Haggling | Building numbers (na/n’, tens, hundreds, thousands, omutwalo) |
| 5 Time and days | Today and tomorrow, Days of the week, Telling the time | Days of the week, Ugandan time (hour one = 7 am) |
| 6 Places and directions | Around town, Going places, Directions | ŋ and Ŋŋenda, ku vs mu, liri/kiri wa |
| 7 The home | Rooms, Welcoming guests, Around the house | Arriving (Kkodi!), commands and “me” prefixes |
| 8 Health and the body | The body, Feeling unwell, At the doctor | “It hurts me” agreement (gunnuma, lunnuma, kunnuma) |

- New stories: *A boda boda ride* (Unit 6) and *At the clinic* (Unit 8).
- Dialogues in units 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8.
- Skills with tips link to their guidebook section from the path.
- **ŋ**: answers now treat `ŋ` as `ng`, and the Ŋŋenda forms also accept
  "Ngenda" as an alternative, for keyboards without ŋ.
- New `plus` icon for the At the doctor skill.
- New test: every lesson builds at least 8 exercises with 3 different
  seeds, and every unit's checkpoint builds (99 tests in total).
- axe found no issues on the path, the new guidebooks, the stories and a
  Unit 6 lesson. A keyboard-only run of the boda story and a Unit 6
  practice session completed.

## Native-speaker review checklist (Units 3–8)

- [ ] Food words: *ebijanjaalo*, *ekyennyanja*, *enva*, *emicungwa*,
      *ennanaasi*, *amenvu*; *Kiwooma nnyo*, *Caayi ne sukaali*.
- [ ] Numbers: *kkumi n’emu*, *amakumi abiri/asatu/ataano*, *bikumi
      bibiri/bitaano*, *lukumi*, *nkumi bbiri/ttaano*, *Omutwalo gumu*.
- [ ] Haggling: *Kino ssente mmeka?*, *Kya bbeeyi*, *Kya buseere*,
      *Kendeezaako*, *Kale, nkitwala*.
- [ ] Time: *jjo* (both meanings), *ku makya*, *olweggulo*, day names,
      *Ssaawa mmeka?*, Ugandan hours.
- [ ] Places: *Ŋŋenda ku ssomero* vs *mu kibuga*, *Eddwaliro liri wa?*,
      *Kiri kumpi/wala*, *Ntwala*, *Yimirira wano*.
- [ ] Home: *Ennyumba yange*, *Maama ali mu ffumbiro*, *Kkodi*,
      *Tusanyuse okukulaba*, *Ggalawo/Ggulawo*, *Ndeetera*, *Ssaako
      ettaala*, *Bambi*.
- [ ] Health: *Omutwe gunnuma*, *Olubuto lunnuma*, *Okugulu kunnuma*,
      *Amaaso gange*, *Nnina ssennyiga/omusujja*, *Wona mangu*.
- [ ] Stories: *Nkumi ssatu*, *Ndaga ekkubo*, *Olina omusujja?*, *Nywa
      eddagala lino, owummule*.
