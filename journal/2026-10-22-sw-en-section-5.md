  - There’s been an accident; Don’t…!; emergency numbers by country
  - May I…? / Could I…?; Thank you for + noun or -ing
  - He said, “…” and reported speech; the moral of the story

- Dropped as same-in-English or already taught: Paukwa, Pakawa, Abunuwasi, lesson (funzo), clever (mjanja).
- Path headings: sw-en labels in English and Luganda cover all 5 sections.
