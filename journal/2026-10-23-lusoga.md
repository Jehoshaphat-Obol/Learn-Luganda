## How the Lusoga was drafted

Lusoga and Luganda are close, so the units were drafted from the Luganda pack
through the regular correspondences between the two, then a word list for the
places where Lusoga uses another word. The tooling is in the project handoff
folder `handoff/xog/` (`soga.py` rules and word list, `draft.py` units,
`derive.py` notes, `extra.txt` story answers the Luganda pack leaves out,
`check.mjs` compose check).

- No doubled consonants: emmere → emere, ssente → sente, ennamba → enamba.
- Luganda zz and jj become dh: amazzi → amaadhi, omusajja → omusadha,
  jjajja → dhaadha.
- Initial doubled consonants become i-: ggwe/ffe/mmwe → iwe/ife/imwe,
  bbiri/ssatu/nnya/ttaano/kkumi → ibiri/isatu/ina/itaano/ikumi.
- Word list: nnyo → inho, kati → atyanu, leero → lero, jjo → idho,
  okulaba → okubona (see), okusanyuka → okusangaala (be happy),
  wasuze → wasuuze, nti → ti.

Notes keep English prose and only change the Luganda words inside it. Tips
that were about Luganda spelling were rewritten (u1 long sounds, u10 noun
class, u2 Olusoga/Musoga/Busoga). Unit content that is about Buganda (clans,
the Kabaka, Kasubi, Namugongo) stays as it is, because the units share one
curriculum; a Busoga version of units 25–26 (Kyabazinga, Busoga clans) is a
good follow-up once a reviewer is involved.

## For the reviewer

Anything that still looks like Luganda probably is: the rules only catch
regular differences. These are the doubts I'd check first:

- Greetings: Oli otya / Gyendi / Wasuuze otya as the Lusoga forms, Weeraba
  and Tulibonagana for goodbye, Ee for yes, Nedda for no.
- Whether Lusoga writes "nh" (enhumba, inho) or "ny" (enyumba). I kept "ny"
  everywhere except inho.
- Verbs that may differ more than by sound: -yiga (learn), -genda/-ja (go),
  -jja/-idha (come), -agala/-enda (want, love).
- Kinship and respect terms (dhaadha, sebo, nyabo, ssenga).
- Proverbs in u36 are Luganda proverbs in Lusoga spelling; Busoga has its own.
- The Runyankore, Swahili and French landing copy about Lusoga.
