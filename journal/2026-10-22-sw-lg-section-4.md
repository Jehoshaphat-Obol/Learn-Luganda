- Swahili tips about Luganda:
  - "of" -a agrees with the owned noun, like Swahili wa/cha/ya/vya (ekika kya ≈ … cha, ebitabo bya ≈ vitabu vya)
  - applicative -ir-/-er- ≈ Swahili -i-/-e- (limira = limia, fumbira = pikia, kolera = fanyia kazi)
  - Nkwagaliza / Mbaagaliza: object markers ku/ba ≈ ninakutakia / ninawatakia; Kulika ≈ hongera
  - relatives: Luganda repeats the initial vowel (abantu abayimba) where Swahili adds -o- (wanaoimba)
  - holiday greetings with -lungi ≈ Krismasi njema; Nsaba = ninasali / naomba
  - causative -is-/-es- ≈ -ish-/-esh- (somesa = somesha, lisa = lisha, nywesa = nywesha)
  - locatives mu/ku/e vs Swahili mu-/ku-/pa- classes and -ni (mu kibira = msituni)
- Culture notes point to East African counterparts (chama/upatu savings groups, Chagga/Haya banana-coffee gardens, Nyanza = Lake Victoria, harusi committees, pilgrims to Namugongo from Kenya and Tanzania).
