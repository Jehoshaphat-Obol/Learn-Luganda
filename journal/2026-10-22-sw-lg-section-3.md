- Swahili tips about Luganda:
  - negation: si-/to-/ta-/tetu- ≈ si-/hu-/ha-/hatu- (simanyi = sijui, sirina = sina, tewali = hakuna); Togenda! ≈ Usiende!
  - questions keep statement order with the question word where the answer goes, as in Swahili (Ogenda wa? = Unakwenda wapi?); oba = au, mpola ≈ polepole
  - today’s past (-dde/-ze/-ye) ≈ -me-; far past na- ≈ -li-; nkyali ≈ ningali
  - near future nja ku- vs Swahili -ta-; warning that Luganda -li- is future while Swahili -li- is past
  - feelings in the perfect: nkooye = nimechoka, nsanyuse = nimefurahi; Bambi ≈ pole, Nsonyiwa ≈ samahani
  - object markers inside the verb: n-/-ku-/-mu-/-tu-/-ba- ≈ -ni-/-ku-/-m-/-tu-/-wa-
  - -ko “a bit” on verbs (Gattako, Kendeezaako); Ka tulye ≈ tule
- Culture notes point to Swahili counterparts where useful (Inshallah / Mungu akipenda, Harambee Stars / Taifa Stars, bao).
