- Grammar tips:
  - negatives of the present (sitaki, hutaki, hataki…), si for "is not", and the past and future negatives (sikuenda, sitaenda)
  - question words staying in place, je for yes-or-no questions, and kwa nini / kwa sababu
  - -me- for things done and its negative -ja- (bado sijala), and the -li- past for every person
  - -ta- for every person, and -nge- "would" (ningependa)
  - feelings you have (nina furaha) and feelings as -me- verbs (nimefurahi), plus short na- forms
  - ku- infinitives (ninapenda kuimba)
  - object markers -ni-, -ku- and -m- (nipigie, nitakupigia)
  - the -ni locative (motoni, sokoni) and recipe verbs with -sha
- Culture notes:
  - declining politely
  - Mungu akipenda
  - pole and hongera
  - Simba vs Yanga
  - bongo flava
  - M-Pesa
  - the three words for rice
- I left "je" out as a vocabulary item, since its English gloss would only be "(question)". The tip and sentences teach it instead.
