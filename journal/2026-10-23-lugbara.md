## How it is drafted

Lugbara is a Central Sudanic language, unrelated to the Bantu, Luo and Ateso packs, so every line is translated from the English curriculum by Claude, with low confidence; the course is marked `draft`. The grammar and word choices are listed in the project handoff folder (`handoff/lgg/GLOSSARY.md`) so they stay consistent: pronouns *ma, mi, eri, ama, emi, yi* placed before the noun as possessives, *ra* for done actions, *ka* for the future and for “if”, sentence-final *ku* for negation and *ya* for yes/no questions.

## Doubts for a native-speaker review

- Basic words chosen with low confidence: *Ku* for “no”, *ndrani/drusi/ndrozi* (today/tomorrow/yesterday), the plural suffix *-yi*, weekdays as *Etu alu … Etu azya*, and hours counted from sunrise (*sawa alu* = 7 o’clock).
- Many words in later units are guesses or loans (*suru* clan, *ozu* totem, *opi* chief/king, *ota* wedding, *o'bi* hare, *ondru* hyena, *inzita* respect). The Luganda proverbs in u36 are translated literally.
- Apostrophes are typed as plain `'` in the units; answer checking treats ’ and ' the same.
